# release-management/RM Sig

The main responsibility of this SIG group is to coordinate Maintainers, QA, and other teams from various development SIGs in the community to complete the release work of openFuyao community versions. The main responsibilities are as follows:

**Maintainer responsibilities include but are not limited to the following:**

- Plan the release timeline for openFuyao versions.
- Track development status (updating patches or feature features) during development/testing cycles.
- Release coordination (within QA, release engineers, technical committee, etc.), participate in relevant groups and release-related meetings.
- Responsible for coordination of openFuyao project delivery process.
- Version review and release decision-making.

**Committer responsibilities include but are not limited to the following:**

- Synchronize version planning/schedules.
- Nominate/recommend new features.
- Track feature delivery progress.
- Review new features.
- Review version quality.
- Write or update version-related documentation.
- Optimize version management processes.
- Organize offline version-related activities.

## maintainer election process and principles

Nominated by maintainer and extended members (individuals first apply to the above members), reviewed by RM sig.

- Committer members have made continuous contributions to the community for more than 6 consecutive months, and at least one feature is a key feature of the version (exists in release notes).
- Committer members continuously participate in RM sig regular meetings, with an attendance rate of over 70% in the past 6 months.
- Committer members representing partner teams can independently participate in community decision-making.
- Individuals and organizations who have made outstanding contributions to release management/community version operations can self-nominate (self-provide evidence of contributions).

## maintainer change process and principles

- Voluntary withdrawal or release proposes withdrawal, RM sig initiates update after review.
- Voluntary withdrawal or failure to participate in RM sig regular meetings for more than 6 consecutive times without reason.
- Individual or affiliated team has no community contributions for 6 consecutive months.

## committer change process and principles

- Voluntary withdrawal or release proposes withdrawal, RM sig initiates update after review.
- Voluntary withdrawal or failure to participate in RM sig regular meetings for more than 6 consecutive times without reason.
- Individual or affiliated team has no community contributions for 6 consecutive months.

## committer addition process and principles

- Active nomination by various partner teams, regular participation in RM sig group regular meetings, participation in version planning, scheduling, review, decision-making and other processes.
- Individuals and organizations who have made outstanding contributions to this sig/community version operations can self-nominate (self-provide evidence of contribution content, contribution form is not limited).
